Build a complete, modern, responsive **personal GATE Examination Preparation Website**.

This is a **private/personal study platform**, not a public coaching website.

## 1. MAIN PURPOSE

The website should organize my complete GATE preparation material in a highly structured way:

* GATE syllabus
* Subject-wise notes
* Topic-wise notes
* Previous Year Question Papers
* Year-wise question papers
* Question-wise answers
* Detailed solutions/explanations
* Important formulas
* Important concepts
* Revision material
* Practice questions
* Progress tracking
* Search across all study material

Everything must follow the official GATE syllabus hierarchy.

The user should never feel that notes or questions are randomly scattered.
